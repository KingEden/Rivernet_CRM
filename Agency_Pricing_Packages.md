## Package Overview

### 1. Starter Website
> **Perfect for:** Startups, local businesses, freelancers, and professionals looking to establish a strong, credible online presence.

#### What's Included:
* **Pages & Design:** Up to 5 Custom Designed Pages with Fully Responsive Design (Desktop, Tablet & Mobile).
* **Copywriting & Content:** Professional Copywriting for 2 Core Pages.
* **Integrations:** Contact Form, Google Maps Integration, and Social Media Integration.
* **SEO & Visibility:** Basic On-Page SEO, Google Analytics Setup, and Google Search Console Setup.
* **Technical & Security:** Domain & Hosting Configuration, SSL Security Certificate, and Basic Speed Optimization.
* **Support:** 30 Days Post-Launch Support.

#### Expected Outcome:
> Launch a modern, professional website that builds trust, showcases your business, and provides a strong digital foundation.

---

### 2. Professional Website ⭐ *(Most Popular)*
> **Perfect for:** Growing businesses looking to generate leads, improve search visibility, and provide a seamless customer experience.

#### Everything in Starter, Plus:
* **Pages & CMS:** Up to 10 Custom Designed Pages with a User-Friendly CMS for Easy Content Updates and Blog Setup.
* **Branding:** Professional Logo Design and Complete Brand Identity Kit.
* **Copywriting:** Professional Copywriting for Up to 10 Pages.
* **SEO & Search Growth:** Advanced Technical & On-Page SEO and Google Business Profile Setup.
* **Conversion Tools:** Online Booking & Appointment System, Advanced Contact Forms, and Analytics Dashboard.
* **Integrations & Performance:** Limited Custom Integrations and Enhanced Website Performance Optimization.
* **Support:** 60 Days Post-Launch Support.

#### Expected Outcome:
> Build a professional digital presence that attracts more customers, ranks higher on search engines, and converts visitors into qualified leads.

---

### 3. Premium Website
> **Perfect for:** High-growth businesses, enterprise brands, e-commerce companies, and organizations requiring a complete digital ecosystem.

#### Everything in Professional, Plus:
* **Unlimited Scale:** Unlimited Custom Pages, Unlimited Professional Copywriting, and Fully Custom UI/UX Design.
* **Media Production:** On-Location Professional Photography and Corporate Video Production.
* **Automation & E-Commerce:** AI Chatbot Integration, Complete E-Commerce Store Build, and Membership & Client Portal.
* **Integrations:** Unlimited Custom API & Tool Integrations.
* **Marketing Infrastructure:** Full SEO Campaign Setup, PPC Advertising Setup, Social Media Management Setup, and Email Marketing Automation.
* **Analytics & Performance:** Custom Analytics Dashboard, Enterprise Security Hardening, and Premium Performance Optimization.
* **Maintenance & Support:** 3 Months Website Maintenance Included, 3 Months Website Care Plan Included, Priority Technical Support, and 90 Days Post-Launch Support.

#### Expected Outcome:
> Create a scalable, automated digital platform that increases revenue, improves customer engagement, and drives long-term business growth.

## Strategic Upgrade Pathways

* **Starter → Professional:** Ideal when a client transitions from needing basic online validation to actively capturing leads and running inbound content/SEO marketing.
* **Professional → Premium:** Ideal when a business scales up operations requiring e-commerce, custom customer portals, automated chat/marketing funnels, and enterprise-grade security with ongoing maintenance.
